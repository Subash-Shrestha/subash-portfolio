About & Value

My interest in digital marketing started with a simple question: why do some campaigns convert and others don't? That question turned into a career built around data and analytics — using audience insights, targeting and creative testing to work out not just how to get a brand in front of people, but in front of the right people, and to turn that attention into leads, sales and repeat customers.

Four years in, that's still what pulls me in: watching a dashboard tell you a story about a campaign, and using that story to shape the next hook, the next audience segment, the next ad. I've managed social accounts end-to-end, run Meta Ads lead-gen and awareness flights, filmed and edited 500+ Reels, and reported results back in language non-marketers can act on — always with the same goal: make the data work harder for the brand it's attached to.

New to the Sydney market, not new to the work — and still hungry to learn how it's done here.
